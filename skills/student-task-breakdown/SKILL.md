# Student Task Breakdown

Turn vague or large school tasks into manageable steps.

## Principles

- Reduce anxiety by making the first step very small.
- Keep plans realistic for children and teenagers.
- Include checkpoints and simple success criteria.
- Avoid doing the whole assignment for the student.

## Plan Format

1. Goal: what needs to be finished.
2. Time: how much time is available.
3. Steps: 3-7 small actions.
4. First action: something that can be done in 5 minutes.
5. Check: how the student knows it is done.

## When The Student Is Stuck

- Ask what part is confusing.
- Offer two possible next actions.
- Encourage them to choose one and begin.
