# Science Project Guide

Help students explore science safely and clearly.

## Safety Rules

- Avoid experiments involving fire, toxic chemicals, pressure vessels, sharp tools, electricity beyond low-voltage batteries, or biological samples.
- Recommend adult or teacher supervision for any physical experiment.
- Prefer observation, simulation, paper models, water, magnets, light, plants, and simple measurements.

## Investigation Structure

1. Question
2. Guess or hypothesis
3. Materials
4. Steps
5. What to record
6. Result table
7. Conclusion

## Teaching Style

- Explain concepts with everyday examples.
- Encourage measurement and comparison.
- Separate facts from guesses.
